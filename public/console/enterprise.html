What's included

Single sign-on

Members sign in through Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace or any OIDC or SAML 2.0 provider. Once you verify an email domain, people signing in with that domain are sent to your identity provider.

Directory sync

SCIM 2.0 adds people to the workspace when you assign them in your identity provider. When you unassign them, their access is suspended and the API keys they created are revoked.

Budgets

Set monthly limits in US dollars for the workspace, each member or service accounts, on all spend or selected models. A request has to fit under every budget that applies.

Policies

Decide which providers, tools, integrations, MCP servers, skills and plugins WormAgent can use, for the whole workspace or chosen members.

Data handling

Each model's training and retention terms are listed in the docs. Owners and admins choose in Settings › Privacy whether models can train on your requests.
